# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Hiring managers, engineering leaders, and founders evaluating Igor for a
  Principal/Staff frontend or product engineering role, or for contract work.
  They arrive from LinkedIn, a referral, a CV link, or the blog, skim for a
  minute or less, and decide whether to reach out.
- Secondary: readers of blog.igorcodes.dev who want to know who writes it.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that makes the case for hiring or contracting Igor
Cheliadinski and turns that case into an email. Success is a qualified
conversation started from the contact links (email first).

Wanted opportunities, all confirmed: AI product engineering, frontend
architecture, and accessibility-grade product work. Open to both full-time and
contract.

## Positioning

A frontend-rooted engineer with a systems mindset: "outcomes, not stacks".
Twelve years (since 2014) from Software Engineer to Principal Engineer. The
proof is range under one judgment: web AR try-on (WebGL, MediaPipe, WASM),
realtime voice-AI workplace simulations, a WCAG AAA-targeted checkout,
Fortune 500 platforms with distributed teams, company-wide AI adoption, and now
his own products: Loremark (web + iPhone, three.js, AI-assisted catalog) and
Temper (iPhone + Apple Watch training timer).

## Operating Context

- Based in Seattle, WA.
- Writes "Engineering Notes" at blog.igorcodes.dev (software architecture,
  frontend engineering, AI systems); the home page pulls its JSON feed.
- Contact: hi@igorcodes.dev, GitHub, LinkedIn, Telegram.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Eleventy 3 static site on Vercel; static-first, no client framework; build-
  time rendering preferred over client JS (see AGENTS.md).
- Licensed fonts Wotfard and Dank Mono stay.
- Shared with the blog and kept stable: `fm-` class prefix, `personal-site-*`
  filenames, `fm-theme` localStorage key, token names. The home page may
  diverge visually from the blog.
- Dark and light themes both ship.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: igorcodes / Igor Cheliadinski.
- Voice: plain, outcome-focused, unhyped ("I focus on outcomes, not stacks").

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: show the range of real work, not skill lists.
2. One action: every path leads to starting a conversation.
3. Judgment is the product: the site itself should read as considered,
   fast, and accessible, because that is what is being hired.
4. Respect a one-minute skim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is a claimed specialty (WCAG AAA checkout work), so the site must
meet at least WCAG 2.2 AA, with full keyboard support and reduced-motion
handling.
